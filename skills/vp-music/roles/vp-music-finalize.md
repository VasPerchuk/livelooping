# vp-music-finalize

Follow `skills\vp-music\CONTRACT.md` and the finalization and worksheet skills.
Read research citations, initial chords, all active instrument pattern files
and the internal draft (or legacy root Draft worksheet). Confirm section order,
bar/second calculations, pattern references and
the three-track RC-300 constraint if live audio layers are assigned.

Provide practical guitar fingerings ahead of section-wise chord material in
`chords.md`; name the tuning/capo and mark alternative shapes where ambiguous.
Full lyrics are only included when supplied by the user or appropriately licensed;
otherwise link to the source and retain a chord chart without pretending it has
lyrics. Publish a concise root `worksheet.md` with a `Transition:` action at every
section, not the draft's full justification. Flag simultaneous actions a
single performer cannot execute as `INFEASIBLE` and add an explicit final
transition warning; a warned Final is allowed but not fully executable.
Leave the draft unpromoted if essential song components are missing.
Report the resulting files, caveats and up to three
actions for rehearsal or correction.
